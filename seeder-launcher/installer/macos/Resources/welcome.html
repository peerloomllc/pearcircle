PearCircle Seeder

Installs an always-on blind-seeder daemon that replicates encrypted PearCircle circle data without holding the decryption key. After install:

  • The daemon launches automatically at login and survives logout.
  • The monitoring UI opens automatically in your default browser.
  • If you missed it, the URL is logged to ~/Library/Application Support/PearCircle Seeder/seeder.log (grep for UI at).

To enroll in a circle, paste a seed invite link from a PearCircle member into the UI.
